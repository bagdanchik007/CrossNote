Transparency

Privacy during development

CrossNote is an early prototype. This page describes the current feature set; it is not a complete privacy notice for a future online service.

Desktop prototype

The app currently has no user accounts or online sync. Note files are stored in the vault you select. The search index and local settings are kept in your device's application data directory.

The app currently has no telemetry or analytics feature. Backups, sync through other software, and your vault's storage location are also subject to the settings of those programs and services.

Project website

The static CrossNote pages do not load analytics scripts, ad networks, or external fonts. Your hosting provider may process technical access data; consult its privacy information for details.

Future online features

Before adding accounts or online sync, we will document data flows, retention, security measures, and ways to export data and delete an account.
